Your adaptive preparation plan

Three clear phases

Paper rhythm

Rotate all four papers each week. Give Accounting and Quantitative Aptitude regular problem-solving sessions; use spaced recall and MCQs for Business Laws and Business Economics. Put your weakest paper in your freshest study block.

Mock plan

  • Use current ICAI material, RTP/MTP and past papers.
  • Start full mocks at 2:00 PM to match the announced exam start.
  • Review every mock separately; classify mistakes as concept, calculation, memory or timing.
  • Use the final days for paper-order revision, essentials and rest.
A planner supports your preparation; it cannot guarantee results. Your teachers and ICAI’s current announcements remain the authority for examinable coverage.
